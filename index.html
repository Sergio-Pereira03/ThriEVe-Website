What is the soul of a car? It's NOT the engine. That may be the heart, but the soul? The soul of the car is the tactile, immersive experience of “shifting” through the different gears... the different performance experiences, driving YOUR machine the way YOU want to!

  • The hardware: a sim-racing-grade H-pattern shifter, hand-built by a specialist in Wales
  • The interface: reads every shift and speaks to the car's electric drivetrain in real time
  • The software: gives each gear its own torque, response, and character

The seat hugs you just right. The leather or hardwood wheel falls into your hands, the analog gauges dance, and the custom, one-of-a-kind shift knob sits right where it should be.

You press the EV clutch, slot into first, and squeeze the throttle. Instant torque throws you back into your seat. Second, third: effortless. Fourth, smooth. Fifth, no drag, gliding silently over the open road. Roll back down to fourth, into third, and the strong, smooth regenerative braking slows you just enough for the curve. You're in total control. Into second, the regen braking throws you into your seatbelt. You still haven't touched the brakes. Into the apex, accelerator down: the tires grip the improved EV balance of the chassis, and you fire out of the corner without breaking a sweat.

You're finally driving again. You're seventeen again. You're ThriEVing.

05 — The interface

Our product: the interface.

We build the brain that makes the shifter real. Our interface reads your clutch and gear, then transmits YOUR DESIRES to the drivetrain instantaneously. So, YOU “shift” how the motor behaves and the car performs.

01

Read

The interface reads your clutch and gear position from the shifter, in real time.

02

Translate

Each gear is fully customizable and mapped to its own torque curve, RPM range and regen braking level.

03

Deliver

The motor answers your hand: instant torque and acceleration, massive regen-engine braking at your fingertips. A drivetrain that responds immediately to the gear you chose. You may never do a brake job again!

  • Drive-by-wire — the shifter is electronically mapped to the motor
  • Tuned per gear — each gear gets its own feel and power delivery
  • This is the product — the interface, paired with the shifter, is what we make
