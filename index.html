Life

01 — The revival

EV brought them back to life.

Electric power gave classic cars a second life: clean, quick, reliable, ready to drive every day. The hard part was solved.

Classic heart, electric soul.

Lost

02 — What we lost

But we lost the shifter.

Somewhere in the swap, the gear lever disappeared. One speed, nothing to row, nothing to decide. The car got faster and quieter — and the driver got left out.

Row

03 — The shifter

A real H-pattern, in your hand.

The hardware is a sim-racing-grade H-pattern shifter, hand-built by a specialist in Wales. Proper gates, weighted detents, a real mechanical throw. It's the tactile half of the connection — the part you grab.

[ The shifter — photo / render ]
  • True H-pattern gate — row real gear positions by hand, no paddles
  • Sim-racing pedigree — built for feel by a specialist shifter maker in Wales
  • Mechanical throw — weighted gates and detents you can feel
  • Cabin-mounted — sits where a gear lever belongs, in reach of your hand

Made to be shifted. Made to be driven.

Link

04 — The interface

Our product: the interface.

ThriEVe builds the brain that makes the shifter real. Our interface reads the gear you select and speaks to the car's electric drivetrain, so a shift genuinely changes how the motor behaves. No clutch, two pedals, all engagement.

01

Read

The interface reads your gear position from the shifter, in real time.

02

Translate

Each gear is mapped to its own torque curve and response — a distinct character, top to bottom.

03

Deliver

The motor answers your hand: engine-braking feel on downshifts, instant torque underneath, a drivetrain that responds to the gear you chose.

  • Drive-by-wire — the shifter is electronically mapped to the motor
  • Tuned per gear — each gear gets its own feel and power delivery
  • This is the product — the interface, paired with the shifter, is what ThriEVe makes

You choose. You shift. You're in control.
